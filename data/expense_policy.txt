NEXUS CORP - TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
Effective Date: April 1, 2026
Version 5.1

1. POLICY OVERVIEW
This Travel and Expense Policy governs the reimbursement of business-related travel, accommodation, meals, entertainment, and other expenses incurred by Nexus Corp employees. The policy ensures fiscal responsibility, fairness, and compliance with tax regulations. Employees must obtain prior authorization for travel and adhere to expense submission deadlines. Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving expenses within their budgets.

2. TRAVEL AUTHORIZATION
Domestic travel exceeding $500 and all international travel require pre-approval through the Concur platform. Requests must include purpose, dates, destinations, estimated costs, and business justification. Travel on weekends requires manager approval unless it results in net cost savings. Employees may combine personal travel with business travel only if personal expenses are segregated and do not increase company costs. First-class and business-class airfare are permitted only for flights exceeding 8 hours with VP approval.

3. TRANSPORTATION
Economy or premium economy class is the default for air travel. Trains may be booked in business class for journeys exceeding 4 hours. Rental cars should be compact or midsize unless carrying multiple passengers or equipment. Ride-sharing services such as Uber and Lyft are reimbursable for airport transfers and local travel. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the IRS standard mileage rate. Tolls and parking are reimbursable with receipts. Traffic violations and parking tickets are the employee's responsibility.

4. ACCOMMODATION
Hotels should be selected based on location, safety, and corporate rates. A nightly rate cap of $350 applies in major metropolitan areas and $250 elsewhere. Booking must be made through the corporate travel portal when possible. Incidental charges such as minibar, pay-per-view, and personal services are not reimbursable. Early check-in and late check-out are reimbursable if they extend the stay by more than 4 hours for business purposes.

5. MEALS AND INCIDENTALS
Per diem rates apply when itemized receipts are not submitted. Domestic per diem is $75 per day for meals and incidentals, allocated as breakfast $15, lunch $25, and dinner $35. International per diem varies by destination and is published in the Global Rates Guide. Alcohol is reimbursable only with client entertainment and requires itemized receipt and business justification. Tips up to 15% are reimbursable. Room service during illness is reimbursable with manager approval.

6. ENTERTAINMENT AND CLIENT MEALS
Client meals and entertainment are reimbursable when directly related to business development or relationship management. Alcohol is limited to two beverages per person. Entertainment events such as concerts and sporting events require VP approval and must be logged in the client relationship management system. Gift expenses are capped at $50 per person annually and must include recipient information and business purpose. Holiday gifts to clients require pre-approval from Marketing.

7. EXPENSE SUBMISSION
Expenses must be submitted through Concur within 30 days of the transaction date. Receipts are required for all expenses exceeding $25. Credit card statements are not acceptable as receipts. Expenses submitted after 30 days require CFO approval and may be delayed for tax reporting purposes. Personal expenses accidentally charged to the corporate card must be reimbursed to the company within 5 business days. Expense disputes should be directed to the Finance help desk.

8. ADVANCES AND CORPORATE CARDS
Travel advances are available for international trips exceeding 7 days and must be reconciled within 14 days of return. Corporate cards are issued to employees with frequent travel or procurement responsibilities. Cards must be used exclusively for business expenses. Monthly statements must be reviewed and approved by the cardholder within 5 business days. Late payments incur a 1.5% monthly interest charge to the employee. Lost cards must be reported immediately.

9. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
The following expenses are not reimbursable: personal grooming and clothing unless required for safety; fitness center fees; family travel expenses; in-room movies and video games; traffic and parking fines; interest on personal credit cards; currency conversion fees on personal cards; and expenses related to canceled trips unless cancellation was for company business. Employees should consult the Finance team when in doubt.

10. AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE
Expense reports are subject to random and risk-based audits by the Internal Audit team. Misrepresentation of expenses constitutes fraud and may result in termination and legal action. Tax regulations require proper documentation for all reimbursements. The Finance team publishes quarterly compliance reports highlighting common errors and policy updates. Managers who approve fraudulent expenses share accountability.

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to the Finance Operations team at finance-ops@nexuscorp.example or ext. 3200.
